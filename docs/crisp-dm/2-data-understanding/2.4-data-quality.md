Both caveats are addressable during Data Preparation (Phase 3) and do not require additional data collection.

### Missingness Patterns

**Age missingness is MAR (Missing At Random conditioned on observed variables):**
- Age-missing passengers have lower survival (29% vs 41%), higher Pclass (mean 2.60 vs 2.24), lower fare (mean 22.16 vs 34.69), and higher male proportion (70.1% vs 63.4%).
- Chi-squared test confirms Age missingness is significantly associated with Sex and Pclass.
- This is not MCAR — naive mean/median imputation would introduce bias. Title-group medians are recommended (Mr: ~30, Mrs: ~36, Miss: ~22, Master: ~4).

**Cabin missingness is MNAR (Missing Not At Random):**
- Strongly stratified by class: 1st class 18.5% missing, 2nd class 91.3% missing, 3rd class 97.6% missing.
- Missingness itself is informative (correlates with lower class and lower survival). A HasCabin indicator captures this signal.

**Row-level completeness (train.csv):**
- 183 rows (20.5%) fully complete (no missing values)
- 550 rows (61.7%) missing exactly 1 field (almost always Cabin)
- 158 rows (17.7%) missing exactly 2 fields (typically Cabin + Age)
- 0 rows with >50% fields missing
