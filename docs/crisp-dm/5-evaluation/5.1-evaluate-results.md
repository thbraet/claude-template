## Business Impact Assessment

### Value Delivered
- **Primary value:** Binary classifier that predicts Titanic passenger survival with 84.73% accuracy, significantly outperforming the gender-only baseline
- **Quantified benefit:** ~25 additional correct predictions out of 418 test passengers compared to the gender baseline (~354 correct vs. ~329)
- **Improvement over current process:** +7.69% accuracy improvement over the gender-only rule; the model captures class, age, family, and title signals that the baseline ignores

### Cost of Errors
| Error Type | Frequency | Business Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| False Positives (predicted survived, actually deceased) | 51 (5.7% of training set) | Over-optimistic; no real-world cost (historical data) | For Kaggle: equally costly as FN |
| False Negatives (predicted deceased, actually survived) | 85 (9.5% of training set) | Conservative bias; misses survivors with atypical profiles | Concentrated in 3rd-class males who survived against the odds |

### Net Assessment
The model delivers clear value for its intended purpose: a Kaggle competition entry and CRISP-DM methodology exercise. The +7.69% accuracy improvement over baseline is substantial and well-supported by cross-validation. As a learning project with no operational deployment, the cost of errors is limited to Kaggle score impact. The secondary value — a complete, documented CRISP-DM pipeline with reusable code patterns — is arguably the more important deliverable and is fully achieved.

## Overall Verdict

### Assessment: Conditionally Ready

**Justification:** XGBoost (tuned, 84.73% OOF accuracy) meets all minimum business success criteria and data mining success criteria. The model significantly outperforms the gender baseline (+7.69%), answers all four business questions (BQ1-BQ4, with BQ4 partially), and all experiments are logged in MLflow. The model is ready for Kaggle submission pending one condition.

**Strengths:**
1. All business success criteria (BSC1-BSC4) passed or likely passed
2. Significant improvement over baseline (+7.69%, ~25 more correct predictions)
3. Feature importance consistent across 3 methods (LR, RF, XGBoost)
4. Full CRISP-DM documentation trail and MLflow experiment logging
5. Stable cross-validation performance (std = 1.69%)

**Weaknesses:**
1. 85% stretch target not reached (84.73% — 0.27% short)
2. Formal feature ablation study (DM4/BQ4) not completed
3. FN bias (85 missed survivors vs 51 false survivors)
4. Moderate overfitting in XGBoost (~7% train-val gap)

**Conditions for Deployment** (Conditionally Ready):
1. Submit XGBoost predictions to Kaggle and confirm test accuracy > 80% (BSC2). If Kaggle accuracy < 80%, consider RF or ensemble submission before concluding.
