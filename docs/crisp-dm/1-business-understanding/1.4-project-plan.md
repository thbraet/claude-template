# 1.4 Project Plan

> **Project:** Titanic Survival Prediction
> **Date:** 2026-03-30
> **CRISP-DM Phase:** 1. Business Understanding
> **Status:** Draft

## Project Overview

- **Business Objective:** Predict Titanic passenger survival to maximize Kaggle accuracy and serve as a CRISP-DM methodology reference
- **Data Mining Goal:** Train a binary classifier achieving >80% accuracy on 418 test passengers, with feature importance analysis and model comparison
- **Estimated Duration:** 2 weeks (2026-03-30 to 2026-04-13)
- **Team Size:** 1 (Thibauld, data scientist)
- **Key Constraint:** Small dataset (891 training rows) limits model complexity; no test-set ground truth available locally

## Risk-Adjusted Timeline

### Critical Path
Stage 1 → Stage 2 → Stage 3 → Stage 4 → Stage 5 → Stage 6. All stages are sequential (sole contributor). The critical path equals the total project duration. Stage 4 (Modeling) is the longest and most variable stage.

### Risk Buffers
| Stage | Base Duration | Buffer | Risk-Adjusted Duration | Rationale |
|-------|-------------|--------|----------------------|-----------|
| Stage 2: Data Understanding | 2 days | 0 days | 2 days | Low risk — well-known dataset with published data dictionary |
| Stage 3: Data Preparation | 2 days | 0.5 days | 2.5 days | Medium risk — Age imputation strategy may need iteration (Risk R4) |
| Stage 4: Modeling | 4 days | 1 day | 5 days | High risk — overfitting on small dataset (Risk R1); may need feature engineering loop |
| Stage 5: Evaluation | 2 days | 0 days | 2 days | Low risk — straightforward evaluation |
| Stage 6: Deployment | 2 days | 0 days | 2 days | Low risk — CSV submission |
| **Total** | **13 days** | **1.5 days** | **14.5 days** | Fits within 2-week window with half-day slack |

### Contingency Triggers
| Risk (from 1.2) | Affected Stage | Trigger Condition | Contingency Action | Impact on Timeline |
|-----------------|---------------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| R1: Overfitting | Stage 4 | CV std > 5% across folds | Increase regularization; reduce feature count; try simpler models | +1 day |
| R2: Feature engineering diminishing returns | Stage 3-4 | No accuracy gain from last 3 features added | Stop engineering; focus on model tuning | 0 days (saves time) |
| R4: Age imputation introduces bias | Stage 3 | CV accuracy drops when Age included vs. excluded | Try multiple imputation strategies; consider dropping Age | +0.5 days |
| R5: CV score doesn't match Kaggle | Stage 5-6 | Kaggle score >3% below CV mean | Investigate overfitting; submit simpler model | +1 day |

## Tool & Technique Assessment

### Candidate Modeling Techniques

| # | Technique | Problem Fit | Interpretability | Data Requirements | Training Time | Pros | Cons |
|---|-----------|------------|-----------------|-------------------|---------------|------|------|
| 1 | Logistic Regression | Good — binary classification baseline | High | Low (handles small datasets well) | Seconds | Interpretable coefficients; strong baseline; fast | Linear decision boundary; may underfit interactions |
| 2 | Random Forest | Excellent — handles mixed types, interactions | Medium | Low-medium | Seconds | Robust to overfitting; feature importances; no scaling needed | Can memorize small datasets; many hyperparameters |
| 3 | Gradient Boosting (GBM) | Excellent — top performer on tabular data | Medium | Low-medium | Seconds | Often best accuracy on tabular; handles missing values (some implementations) | Prone to overfitting on small data; requires careful tuning |
| 4 | SVM (RBF kernel) | Good — effective for small datasets | Low | Requires scaling | Seconds | Good generalization on small datasets; kernel trick captures non-linearity | Black box; sensitive to feature scaling; slow with large datasets |
| 5 | k-Nearest Neighbors | Fair — simple, non-parametric | Medium | Requires scaling | Milliseconds | Simple; no training phase; captures local patterns | Curse of dimensionality; sensitive to irrelevant features |

### Recommended Techniques
- **Baseline:** Logistic Regression — establishes interpretable floor, provides coefficient-based feature importance
- **Primary:** Random Forest + Gradient Boosting (scikit-learn GradientBoostingClassifier) — expected best performers on structured tabular data
- **Stretch:** Soft voting ensemble of top-2 models — if time permits and individual models are sufficiently diverse
- **Rationale:** Tree ensembles consistently dominate Kaggle tabular competitions. Logistic regression provides interpretability and a strong baseline. SVM included for diversity. Deep learning excluded due to small dataset size.
